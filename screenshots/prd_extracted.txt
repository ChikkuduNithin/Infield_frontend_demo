User flow for Coordinators and Managers
Login- Using mobile number and password (Login valid for a week)
Mark “Punch In”- System shall capture geotagged selfie
Select Task
School Visit
Permission to Conduct ISRS Programme
Select School Name (using search option)
Confirmation Date Received- Select from drop down
Yes-Choose Date & Submit- Must Upload the Consent Form signed by school for submission (Details of the school name & ISRC Programme date shall automatically appear on the coordinator calendar/ dashboard as a reminder.
No- Choose Follow-up Date & Submit (Details of the school name & follow up date shall automatically appear on the Coordinator calendar/ dashboard as a reminder. Also, Optional “Remarks” Box shall be provided.
Permission Denied- Fill Mandatory Remarks & Submit 
ISRS Awareness Session
Select School Name (using search option)
Enter “No. of Students Participated”
Upload “Photo of the Awareness Session”- Geotagged, and In Pdf/ Image format
Upload “ISRC Awareness Session Acknowledgement Form”- Form shall be in Pdf/ Image form
Optional “Remarks” Box shall be provided before the “Submit” option
ISRC Collection Drive
Select School Name (using search option) 
Give option “Add Another School”- This enables that ISRC is conducted in parallel in other schools as well with help of volunteers.
Select “ISRC Collection Drive Being Conducted By”, Self or Volunteer 
Upload “Photos of Collection Drive” for respective schools- Geotagged, and In PDF/ IMAGE format
Select the “Franchise for Material Collection” for respective school (using search option)
Enter the “Quantity Collected” for respective school- In Kilograms
Optional “Remarks” Box shall be provided before the “Submit” option
After Submission, Select option for “Collection Drive Completed”, Yes, or No
If “Yes”, enable options for:
Enter “Verified Material Quantity”- In Kilograms, basis the weighbridge receipt (This quantity is always less than the Quantity collected) from franchise
Upload “ISRC Collection Drive Acknowledgement Form- Form shall be in PDF/ IMAGE form. Unless this is not uploaded, all such instances shall be counted and appear on volunteer & manager dashboard as a pending activity.                                             
If “No”, enable option to upload photos of “Collection Drive- Day 2” for respective schools- Geotagged, and In PDF/ IMAGE format
Enter the “Quantity Collected” for respective schools- In Kilograms
Optional “Remarks” Box shall be provided before the “Submit” option
After Submission, enable options for:
Enter “Verified Material Quantity”- In Kilograms, basis the weighbridge receipt (This quantity is always less than the Quantity collected) from franchise
Upload “ISRC Collection Drive Acknowledgement Form- Form shall be in PDF/ IMAGE form. Unless this is not uploaded, all such instances shall be counted and appear on volunteer & manager dashboard as a pending activity
The system shall calculate and store both:
Quantity collected for a particular school during the ISRC drive as sum of day-1 and day-2 quantities.
Quantity verified for a particular school during the ISRC drive as sum of day-1 and day-2 quantities.

Dashboards/ Modules
Roles
Features/ Details Needed
Coordinator
Shall be able to see holiday list
Shall be able to see Monthly Target set by Managers
Shall be able to see the Progress (Actual vs Plan)
Shall be able to see No. of pending activities (List to be opened when clicked)
Shall be able to see his route map
Shall be able to see the calendar with:
Attendance- Present/ Absent/ half day/ full day
Completed Activities in the past
Planned Activities for Today & Future
Calander shall include colour codes for: Today, Holiday, Full day, Half Day, Absent, Leave 
Raise Correction Request under the following categories:
Missed punch in/ out
Changing Absent to Leave (Basis the pre-approval taken from manager)
Option to upload Leave approval (in PDF/ IMAGE format) shall be made mandatory in this case.
Access files in “Downloads” Section
Access individual reports (No. of schools/ colleges covered for awareness/ collection, Material collected, and Monthly attendance report in PDF/ IMAGE format etc).
Manager
Manage Database for Schools, Colleges, and Corporates etc. in the state they operate.
Shall be able to see holiday list
Shall be able to set Monthly Targets
Shall be able to see individual coordinator level Progress (Actual vs Plan) as well as overall team progress
Shall be able to see individual coordinator level pending activities (total no, List to be opened when clicked) as well as overall count/ list of pending activities
Shall be able to see coordinator level route map
Shall be able to see his/ her own as well as coordinator level calendar with:
Attendance- Present/ Absent/ half day/ full day
Completed Activities in the past
Planned Activities for Today & Future
Calander shall include colour codes for: Today, Holiday, Full day, Half Day, Absent, Leave, corrected attendance 
Approve Correction Request by coordinators as well as raise own requests under the following categories:
Missed punch in/ out
Changing Absent to Leave (Basis the pre-approval taken from manager)
Option to upload Leave approval (in PDF/ IMAGE format) shall be made mandatory in this case.
Shall be able to see the NGO Compliance tracker
Access files in “Downloads” Section- standard formats to be used across all NGOs
Access Reports of related to their team members
NGO Admin
Manage Database for Employees
Manage franchise database- Add, remove, and modify.
Assign roles to employees
Update holiday list
Set Monthly Target
See list of employees
Shall be able to see individual coordinator level Progress (Actual vs Plan) as well as overall team progress
Shall be able to see individual coordinator level pending activities (total no, List to be opened when clicked) as well as overall count/ list of pending activities
Shall be able to see coordinator level route map
Shall be able to see individual coordinator level calendar and manager calendar with:
Attendance- Present/ Absent/ half day/ full day
Completed Activities in the past
Planned Activities for Today & Future
Approve Correction Request by manager under the following categories:
Missed punch in/ out
Changing Absent to Leave (Basis the pre-approval taken from manager)
Shall be able to see the NGO Compliance tracker
Access files in “Downloads” Section
Access Reports of related to their team 
WoW Admin
Manage NGOs- Add, Modify, Remove NGOs
Assign states to NGOs
Shall be able to see NGO level holiday list, coordinators/ manager details, schools/ colleges/ corporates etc.
Shall be able to see individual NGO level dashboards for all the NGOs including their targets, performance etc.
Track Consolidated Regulatory Compliance related to NGOs- Renewal of certificates, licenses etc. and highlight potential non-conformities.
Manage files in “Downloads” Section
Access NGO/ individual coordinator level Reports

Reports
Coordinator
Download Monthly Calendar in PDF format with attendance marked in colour
Download Day wise list of activities completed & pending during the selected date range
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates/ households etc. covered, students/ people/ employees educated, and material collected etc. during the selected date range
Manager
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates etc. from data base- Location wise (fields present in the data base for a particular category shall be added in dropdown menu)- Restricted to the state they operate
Download Monthly Calendar in PDF format with attendance marked in colour- for self as well as coordinator
Download Day wise list of activities completed & pending during the selected date range- for self as well as coordinator (individual coordinator level and consolidated as well)
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates/ households etc. covered, students/ people/ employees educated, and material collected etc. during the selected date range (individual coordinator level and consolidated data as well)
NGO Admin
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates etc. from data base- Location wise (fields present in the data base for a particular category shall be added in dropdown menu)- Restricted to the state they operate
Download Monthly Calendar in PDF format with attendance marked in colour- for coordinator as well as manager
Download Day wise list of activities completed & pending during the selected date range- for coordinator as well as manager (individual coordinator level and consolidated as well)
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates/ households etc. covered, students/ people/ employees educated, and material collected etc. during the selected date range (coordinator/ manager level as well as consolidated data)
WoW Admin
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates etc. from data base- Location wise (fields present in the data base for a particular category shall be added in dropdown menu)- Not restricted to the state. Any state they shall choose. 
Download Monthly Calendar in PDF format with attendance marked in colour- for coordinator as well as manager
Download Day wise list of activities completed & pending during the selected date range- for coordinator as well as manager (individual coordinator level and consolidated as well)
Download list of schools/ colleges/ corporates/ households etc. covered, students/ people/ employees educated, and material collected etc. during the selected date range (individual NGO/ coordinator/ manager level as well as consolidated data)
